# OBINexus · MMUKO · Zygote · Wheel

*A soft, protective framework for Children, Neurodivergent People, and Community Order*

---

## 🌱 Purpose

This framework exists to protect children and neurodivergent people in moments when larger systems fail, collapse, or act with force. It uses Igbo concepts of **law (IWU)** and **order (IJI)** to create gentle, predictable protection.

This document avoids stress, pressure, or heavy technical language. It is built with **Friday energy** — soft, kind, and human.

---

## 🌿 Core Concepts

### **OHA — Public / Community Children Protection Law**

Children belong under community shielding. OHA ensures:

* No force or coercion
* No adult burdens
* No exposure to adult conflict
* Safety, rest, and guided growth

### **IWU — LAW (Unchanging)**

These are the core rules that protect children and neurodivergent people. They do not change.

1. A child must never carry adult fear or responsibility.
2. Protection is unconditional.
3. Neurodivergent people receive enhanced shielding.
4. Community safety outweighs individual power.
5. No adult may exert force or pressure against a child.
6. Harm against a neurodivergent person counts doubly in protection response.

### **IJI — ORDER (How Law Is Applied)**

IJI provides structure without cruelty.

* Steps must be clear.
* Enforcement must be gentle.
* No chaos, fear, or unpredictable punishment.
* Systems must support, not harm.

## 🌈 Zygote Principle (Beginning of Rights)

Every child begins life with:

* innocence
* freedom from guilt
* protection from harm
* the right to grow at a natural pace
* emotional and physical safety

A child stops being a child **only when they can carry adult responsibility without harm** — not merely because of age.

---

## 🌼 Neurodivergent 2:1 Protection Rule

Neurodivergent people experience and react to force differently. Because of this:

> Any harm, pressure, or coercion against a neurodivergent person triggers **double protective response**.

This is not punitive — it is **shielding**.

---

## 🌻 Net Force Principle (Emotional Safety Model)

Just as objects with net force = 0 feel no push:

> Children and neurodivergent people have the right to **zero emotional force**.

This means:

* no intimidation
* no pressure
* no verbal force
* no adult demands beyond their capacity

## 🍃 Definition of a Child (README Standard)

> A child is anyone who cannot yet carry the weight of adult responsibility or danger without harm. Safety and stability determine this — not age alone.

Adults who were not protected as children may still work to build these systems, but the protections apply **only to children and neurodivergent individuals in real-time need**.
